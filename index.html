How To Play

Goal: Move all 4 of your tokens from your base to the center Home area.

Starting: You must roll a 6 to move a token out of the base onto the starting square.

Moving: Roll the dice to move a token forward along the path.

Rolling 6: Rolling a 6 gives you an extra turn!

Capturing: Landing exactly on an opponent's token sends it back to its base (unless on a safe star tile).

Safe Zones: Tiles with a star (★) or colored starting tiles are safe. Tokens cannot be captured here.

Winning: You must roll the exact number needed to enter the center Home.
